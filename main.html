AIDA: AI Drowning Assistance

Capstone

Python · Flask · YOLO · OpenCV · ESP32 · HTML/CSS/JS · Research team of 5

An AI and IoT drowning-risk detection assistant for the swimming pool of the Cebu City Sports Center. A camera watches a defined pool area and pose estimation checks swimmer posture. When a possible risk is detected, AIDA triggers a sound alert and a handheld alert unit with vibration and light, sent over a local wireless network so it doesn't depend on the cloud.

Lifeguards stay in control: AIDA supports human monitoring and does not replace it. Our research first studies the pool's actual safety conditions and the needs of pool personnel and adult swimmers, then builds and tests a prototype around those requirements. It connects to UN Sustainable Development Goal 9.

My contribution: I serve as the system administrator for the team. I set up and maintain the system environment the prototype runs on, manage user accounts and access on the web dashboard, and keep the device, network, and software settings consistent between testing and demo runs. I also help document the setup so the rest of the team can test without breaking the environment.

What it covers

  • Research first: Chapters 1 to 3 cover the problem, related studies, and the methodology, built around the safety needs of a real pool.
  • Planned with Agile sprints: planning, design, development, testing, and review for each component.
  • Planned stack: a Python and Flask backend with SQLite, a web dashboard, YOLO and OpenCV for detection, and ESP32 boards for the alert units.
  • Honest limits: it is a prototype, and the study does not claim guaranteed detection or proven accuracy.
Research paper: Chapters 1 to 3 complete

FinTrack Pro

Live

HTML · Tailwind CSS · Chart.js · Lucide icons · JavaScript

A personal finance and inventory app. Create an account, track your spending and savings, and see where your money goes in charts, including a donut chart for expenses. It also has savings categories, spending and investment tabs, a protection tab, and a blue or pink theme switcher.

My contribution: I built the whole app and kept improving it: the glass-style layout, login and registration with localStorage, the charts, and publishing it on GitHub Pages. AI helped with code, and I tested every change myself.

Challenges and what I learned

  • My first deployment failed silently because of mismatched filenames and the wrong folder structure. Now I always double-check paths and letter case.
  • Fixing the background gradient showed me how Tailwind can clash with my own CSS, and how background-attachment: fixed behaves on phones.
  • My editor blocks native confirm() pop-ups, so I built custom modals for registration and confirmations.

In progress

Roblox Studio · Team project · 5 members

A team-built tactical shooter in Roblox Studio, where Task Force goes up against the Syndicate. We shared our progress in a video presentation.

My contribution: I was the project manager for our five-person team. I broke the game into tasks, assigned work based on each member's strengths, and set short deadlines so we always had something to show.
